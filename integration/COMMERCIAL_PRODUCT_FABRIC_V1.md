## Cross-office law

**GAC** owns the customer/commercial front door.  
**AAS** is the technical/automation implementation organ.  
**GAR** is the research/evaluation/evidence organ.  
**GAI** is the training/documentation/publication/knowledge-transfer organ.

A product can originate in any one office while invoking the other three as bounded overlays. No office creates a second delivery runtime.
